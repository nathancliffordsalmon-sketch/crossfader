# Like The Fish's Crossfader

**Your YouTube playlists as one continuous mix.** Songs crossfade into each other — sound and picture — full
screen on any monitor, with Winamp-style visualizers, song facts and a little retro flair.

[ ⬇ Download for Windows (v1.7.3, 120 MB) ]   ← link to the Setup .exe

*Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Free.*

## What it does

- **Paste a playlist, press play.** Copy any public or unlisted playlist link from YouTube or YouTube Music.
  Play one playlist, or blend back and forth between two.
- **Smooth crossfades** between every song, and it skips talking intros, outros and dead air.
- **My playlists** remembers what you've played, so next time it's one click.
- **Winamp-style side visualizers** on 4:3 videos, coloured to match each video.
- **"Did you know?" facts** about the song, the artist, and what was in the news the year it came out —
  including the original songs inside remixes and covers.
- **Retro looks:** Neon 80s, Arcade, Groovy 70s fonts, and a little intro you'll recognise if you played
  PC games in 1990.
- Captions on/off, restart song, back to the start of the playlist, and keyboard controls.

## Installing

1. Download and run `Like-The-Fishs-Crossfader-Setup-1.7.3.exe`.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**
   (new apps show this until enough people have installed them).
3. Paste a playlist link and press **Start the mix**.

**Private playlist won't play?** In YouTube, open the playlist, choose the pencil/settings, and set it to
**Unlisted**. It stays hidden from search, but the app can play it.
