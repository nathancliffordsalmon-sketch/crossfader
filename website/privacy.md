# Privacy Policy — Like The Fish's Crossfader

*Last updated: November 11, 2026*

Like The Fish's Crossfader ("the app") is a free Windows app made by Nathan Salmon (likethefish.ca). It plays
YouTube playlists one after another with crossfades. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services: videos play in YouTube's own embedded player. By using the app you agree to
the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's collection and use of data through the
YouTube player is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app is not
affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything stays on your own computer:

- your settings (crossfade length, fonts, display options, and so on);
- **My playlists** — the playlist links you've played or saved, and the names shown for them;
- a cache of song facts it has looked up, and a log file used to troubleshoot problems.

YouTube's player may also store cookies and similar data on your computer, as described in the Google Privacy
Policy. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own, and the author receives none of this data.

## What the app sends to other services

To do its job, the app sends limited information to these services:

| Service | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube / Google | Playlist and video IDs | To play videos, read playlist names and video shapes (oEmbed), and show thumbnails |
| SponsorBlock (sponsor.ajay.app) | The first characters of a scrambled (hashed) video ID | To find intros, outros and talking parts to skip |
| MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org) | Song and artist names | To find the year a song came out |
| Wikipedia (wikipedia.org) | Song, artist and year names | To show "Did you know?" facts |
| likethefish.ca | A request for a small version file | To tell you when a new version is available |

No personal information (name, email, YouTube account) is sent by the app. Each service handles requests under
its own privacy policy.

## Your choices

- Turn off song facts or SponsorBlock skipping in the app's settings.
- Remove playlists from **My playlists** at any time.
- To delete everything the app has stored, uninstall it and delete the folder `%APPDATA%\Like The Fish's Crossfader`.
- To manage data held by Google, visit [Google's security settings](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
